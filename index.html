Ancient Sound → Modern SoSDT

From the first sound to the focused echo.

In Bön Dzogchen, the natural state shows itself as sound, light, and rays. Today, focused ultrasound is studied in sonodynamic therapy. We hold both with respect.

Mantra, drum and bell, and the bronze bowl soften fixation and open awareness. A dolphin sees with sound. The Sonodynamic Cancer Center at AiYogi.ai Lab is a pioneering program in the USA from ARDTiLYS, AiYogi, and AiYogiLAB, and it brings lineage sound and SoSDT into a single contemplative setting.

Conscious Passage

When the time comes, we stay beside you.

In the Dzogchen tradition, death is not only an ending. It is a profound moment of practice, when the nature of mind may be recognized with great clarity. For anyone who wishes it, Conscious Passage offers loving, steady accompaniment at the end of life, honoring the natural moment, never hastening it or holding it back.

Phowa

Instruction in phowa, the transference of consciousness, practiced in advance with a teacher, so that it is familiar when it is needed.

Bardo guidance

Gentle teachings on the bardos, and readings from the Great Liberation upon Hearing in the Bardo, before, during and after the passage.

Resting in clarity

Daily support for meditation and prayer, so that the mind can rest in its own open, luminous nature.

Comfort care alongside

Quiet rooms, gentle bodywork, and close coordination with your own hospice and palliative care team, so that physical comfort and spiritual practice support each other.

Family & sangha

A welcoming space for loved ones and the sangha to gather, practice together, grieve, and be held, with guidance on how to support the one who is passing.

A lama's presence

Where wished, a lama offers presence, prayers and practice at the bedside, followed by traditional prayers through the forty-nine days.

FAQ

Honest answers

What is SoSDT?

A sensitizer is received first and gathers in the body's most vulnerable cells. Gentle focused ultrasound then awakens it exactly where the sound converges, releasing reactive oxygen inside those cells and acting on their mitochondria. The sound is quiet and non-invasive.

Can I continue my current treatments?

Yes. SoSDT is integrative, and it sits alongside the care you already receive. We're glad to coordinate with your doctors.

How is the sensitizer given?
