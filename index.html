What it is

The short version

  • You take a chlorophyll-based sensitizer.
  • Some protocols add a hyperbaric oxygen session.
  • Then you lie in a warm-water bath while low-intensity ultrasound runs.
  • The idea: the sound activates the chlorophyll, which makes reactive oxygen inside damaged cells.
  • The hope: clear early, senescent or pre-tumor cells before they become a problem.

Where the evidence is

  • It’s experimental.
  • Mouse results look promising.
  • Human data is thin: a few small reports, usually alongside other treatment.
  • Nobody has studied it on senescent cells in people yet.
  • It’s not a cure, and this isn’t medical advice.
